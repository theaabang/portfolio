My role

District 219 (Niles North and Niles West) joined forces in 2003 to create its own Dance Marathon, a completely student-run organization that helps a different charity each year. Through canning, bake sales, events, and fundraisers, members of DM help the charity by raising money all year long.

As PR Executive, I managed a team of five to plan weekly content for Dance Marathon’s Instagram. Our posts promoted fundraisers and events, introduced committees, and shared the charity we were raising money for.

I based Dance Marathon’s brand identity on the colors of that year’s beneficiary, Will’s Place, so every post tied back to the charity we were supporting.
