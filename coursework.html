DTX 495 — Designing with Data

Discovery Brief One: Cycling

In this class, we write “How might we” statements based on data we’re given and data we research ourselves. For this brief, CCRPC and UIUC wanted to increase cycling on and around campus for both confident and traffic-anxious riders. Working individually, I analyzed monthly bicycle counts from four campus counters (Illini Union, Morrow Plots, Armory & Fourth, and Green & Lincoln) from August to November 2025, along with bicycle theft reports from UIPD. From there, I identified one service problem and two priorities for further research.

Note: the clients in this brief are only for this assignment.

What I did

  • Cleaned the counter data, leaving out offline months instead of counting them as zero.
  • Charted monthly counts for each site and compared them across all four.
  • Wrote an assumption and a way to test it for each finding.
  • Framed the problem as a “How might we” question and set two research priorities.
  • Designed the slide deck.
