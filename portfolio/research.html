Creating with Compliance

A STUDENT-LED USABILITY & ENGAGEMENT STUDY

Overview

Alongside a team of three other researchers, I worked together with Prof. Marisa Peacock to see how students would react to materials teaching accesibility in Illinois Advertising. My role in this project was developing data models using Qualtrics to determine how students interacted with course material and creating the research poster for the Undergraduate Research Symposium.

Role: Researcher & Data Analyst


Advisor: Prof. Marisa Peacock


Tools Used: Qualtrics, survey design


Deliverables: Data models, research poster, Undergraduate Research Symposium presentation

Methodology

  • Designed a Qualtrics survey to gather student responses on compliance-aware advertising materials.
  • Ran classroom testing to observe how students engaged with the materials directly.
  • Built data models to analyze trends across the qualitative and quantitative survey results.

The Poster

Presenting at the Symposium

Findings were presented live at the Undergraduate Research Symposium, where the team fielded questions on methodology, results, and future implementation of the compliance-aware materials.
