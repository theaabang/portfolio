My role

As the Events & Social Media Intern for Engineering Undergraduate Research (EUR), I ran the office's Instagram. My job was to help engineering students find research opportunities and feel like research was something they could actually do.

That meant designing the opportunity graphics that announce new openings, rebuilding the weekly story recap so deadlines and events were easy to catch, and leading a student-spotlight collab with Women in Engineering from planning through design.
